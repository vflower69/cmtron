Research Partnerships

Collaborating with universities, CROs, biomarker labs, and ingredient suppliers to advance daily longevity science.

Partnership Types

Cellmetron collaborates with institutions and experts across longevity science, cardiometabolic health, anti‑senescence research, cognitive resilience, clinical development, and regulatory compliance.

  • University Research — longevity pathways, mitochondrial biology, anti‑senescence mechanisms, nutrient synergy, biomarker discovery
  • CRO Clinical Execution — trial operations, participant management, controlled study design, data collection, safety monitoring
  • Biomarker Laboratories — NAD+ quantification, oxidative stress markers, mitochondrial performance, inflammatory biomarkers, senescence‑associated factors (SASP), cognitive‑resilience markers
  • Ingredient Suppliers — clinically aligned nutrients including NR, CoQ10, Taurine, Resveratrol, Myricetin, Ergothioneine, Omega‑3, and Curcumin; purity verification; stability optimization; standardized bioactive compounds

Benefits

Cellmetron partners gain access to a scientifically responsible, clinically aligned longevity platform.

  • Access to the Cellmetron Longevity Formula for research use
  • Funding support for qualified studies
  • Co‑authored publications and scientific outputs
  • Advisory roles within the Cellmetron Longevity Lab
  • Co‑branded research initiatives and public releases
