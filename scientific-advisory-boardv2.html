Board Composition

The Cellmetron Scientific Advisory Board brings together leaders across longevity science, mitochondrial biology, anti‑senescence research, cognitive resilience, nutrition, clinical research, and regulatory compliance.

  • Longevity Researcher — cellular aging, NAD+ pathways, mitochondrial resilience
  • Cardiometabolic Scientist — cardiovascular biology, inflammation balance, lipid metabolism
  • Anti‑Senescence Researcher — senescence markers, mitochondrial protection, cellular longevity
  • Cognitive Longevity Scientist — Ergothioneine pathways, transporter‑protected antioxidants, neuro‑resilience
  • Nutrition Scientist — nutrient synergy, dietary biochemistry, metabolic support
  • Clinical Trial Specialist — study design, biomarker analysis, controlled trials
  • Medical Doctor (Internal Medicine) — safety oversight, real‑world health insights
  • Regulatory Expert — compliance, claims validation, quality assurance

Responsibilities

The Scientific Advisory Board ensures that Cellmetron remains clinically responsible, scientifically aligned, and built for real‑world impact.

  • Review and validate scientific foundations of the 8‑ingredient Longevity Formula
  • Advise on clinical development strategy and study design
  • Oversee safety, tolerability, and long‑term wellness considerations
  • Evaluate research partnerships and innovation initiatives
  • Ensure transparency, integrity, and scientific rigor across all communications
