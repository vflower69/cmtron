Our Commitment

Cellmetron is built on scientific responsibility and complete transparency. Every batch undergoes rigorous third‑party testing to ensure purity, potency, safety, and consistency.

  • Heavy Metal Screening — ensures safe levels of lead, arsenic, cadmium, and mercury
  • Potency Verification — confirms accurate levels of NR, CoQ10, Resveratrol, Curcumin, Omega‑3, and Taurine
  • Stability Testing — validates shelf life, cube integrity, and ingredient preservation
