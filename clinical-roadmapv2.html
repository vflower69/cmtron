Our Clinical Roadmap

Science designed to improve daily life — without changing your routine or your budget.

Overview

Cellmetron’s clinical program evaluates the Longevity Formula through observational studies, biomarker analysis, and controlled trials. Our goal is simple: validate a daily longevity ritual that fits naturally into real life — just like enjoying a cup of the best coffee or tea in the world, but even more affordable.

The formula contains eight clinically aligned nutrients: NR, CoQ10, Taurine, Resveratrol, Myricetin, Ergothioneine, Omega‑3, and Curcumin — each chosen for its role in NAD+ support, mitochondrial energy, antioxidant defense, anti‑senescence pathways, cognitive resilience, and cardiovascular health.

Phase 1 — Pilot Observational Study

Objective: Identify early signals in daily energy, sleep quality, vitality, compliance, and tolerability.

This phase helps us understand how Cellmetron performs in real-world routines — the same routines our customers already live every day. Early observational data also helps refine biomarker selection for later phases.

Phase 2 — Biomarker Feasibility Study

Objective: Measure biological signals related to core longevity pathways:

  • NAD+ metabolism (NR)
  • Mitochondrial ATP output (CoQ10)
  • Metabolic stability & hydration (Taurine)
  • Antioxidant defense & sirtuin activation (Resveratrol)
  • Anti‑senescence markers (Myricetin)
  • Cognitive & functional resilience (Ergothioneine)
  • Cardiovascular markers (Omega‑3)
  • Inflammatory modulation (Curcumin)

This phase evaluates how the formula aligns with biological pathways associated with healthy aging and long-term vitality.

Phase 3 — Controlled Clinical Trial

Objective: Generate high‑quality, publishable data through a rigorously designed clinical study.

This phase provides the strongest level of scientific validation for the Cellmetron Longevity Formula, measuring outcomes across NAD+ levels, mitochondrial function, oxidative stress, inflammation, senescence biomarkers, HRV, sleep quality, cognitive clarity, and daily vitality.

Scientific Integrity

All studies are conducted in partnership with certified laboratories and reviewed by our Scientific Advisory Board. Every phase is designed to ensure safety, transparency, and scientific responsibility.
