Our Commitment

Cellmetron is built on scientific responsibility and complete transparency. Every batch undergoes rigorous third‑party testing to ensure purity, potency, safety, and consistency.

  • Microbial Testing — verifies absence of harmful microorganisms
  • Heavy Metal Screening — ensures safe levels of lead, arsenic, cadmium, and mercury
  • Pesticide & Residue Screening — confirms clean, contaminant‑free raw materials
  • Potency Verification — confirms accurate levels of all eight active ingredients:
    • NR
    • CoQ10
    • Taurine
    • Resveratrol
    • Myricetin
    • Ergothioneine
    • Omega‑3
    • Curcumin
  • Stability Testing — validates shelf life, cube integrity, and ingredient preservation

Testing Partners

All COA testing is performed by certified third‑party laboratories specializing in nutritional biochemistry, microbial analysis, and ingredient verification. These partners help ensure that every cube meets the highest standards of safety and scientific integrity.
