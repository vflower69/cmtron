Scientific Advisory Board

Experts guiding Cellmetron’s research, clinical development, and scientific integrity.

Board Composition

The Cellmetron Scientific Advisory Board brings together leaders across longevity science, clinical research, nutrition, and regulatory compliance.

  • Longevity Researcher — cellular aging, NAD+ pathways, mitochondrial science
  • Nutrition Scientist — nutrient synergy, metabolic stability, dietary biochemistry
  • Clinical Trial Specialist — study design, biomarker analysis, controlled trials
  • Medical Doctor (Internal Medicine) — safety oversight, real‑world health insights
  • Regulatory Expert — compliance, claims validation, quality assurance

Responsibilities

The Scientific Advisory Board ensures that Cellmetron remains clinically responsible, scientifically aligned, and built for real‑world impact.

  • Review and validate formula science
  • Guide clinical protocol development and biomarker selection
  • Oversee safety, tolerability, and long‑term health considerations
  • Evaluate research partnerships and R&D initiatives
  • Ensure transparency, integrity, and scientific rigor across all claims
