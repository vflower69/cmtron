Our Clinical Roadmap

Science that improves daily life.

Overview

Cellmetron’s clinical program evaluates the Longevity Formula through observational studies, biomarker analysis, and controlled trials.

Phase 1 — Pilot Observational Study

Goal: early signals in daily energy, sleep quality, vitality, compliance, and tolerability.

Phase 2 — Biomarker Feasibility Study

Goal: biological signals in NAD+, oxidative stress, HRV, sleep metrics, and metabolic markers.

Phase 3 — Controlled Clinical Trial

Goal: publishable data from a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled study.

Scientific Integrity

All studies are conducted in partnership with certified laboratories and reviewed by our Scientific Advisory Board.
