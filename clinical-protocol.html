Clinical Protocol

IRB‑ready framework for evaluating the Cellmetron Longevity Formula.

Study Title

Evaluation of the Cellmetron Longevity Formula on Cellular Energy, Antioxidant Status, Cardiovascular Markers, and Quality‑of‑Life Metrics in Healthy Adults.

Study Design

Randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled clinical trial. Duration: 12 weeks. Participants: 80–120 healthy adults aged 25–65.

Formula Overview

The Cellmetron Longevity Formula contains six clinically aligned nutrients:

  • NR — NAD+ precursor supporting cellular repair and energy metabolism
  • CoQ10 — mitochondrial cofactor supporting ATP production
  • Resveratrol — polyphenol supporting antioxidant pathways and sirtuin activation
  • Curcumin — anti‑inflammatory compound supporting cellular protection
  • Omega‑3 — plant‑derived fatty acids supporting cardiovascular health
  • Taurine — supports metabolic stability, hydration, and mitochondrial protection

Endpoints

Primary Endpoints:

  • NAD+ levels
  • Oxidative stress biomarkers
  • Mitochondrial function markers
  • Inflammatory markers

Secondary Endpoints:

  • HRV (Heart Rate Variability)
  • Sleep quality
  • Daily energy and vitality
  • Mood and cognitive clarity
  • Compliance and tolerability

Safety & Ethics

All clinical activities follow IRB‑approved protocols, informed consent procedures, and HIPAA‑compliant data handling. Participants undergo weekly safety monitoring, including adverse event tracking and compliance checks.

Data Handling

All data is anonymized, encrypted, and stored in secure research environments. Only authorized clinical personnel may access identifiable information.

Publication Plan

Results will be analyzed using standard statistical methods and prepared for submission to peer‑reviewed journals in nutrition, metabolism, and preventive health.
