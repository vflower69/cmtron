Advancing daily longevity through science, engineering, and human‑centered innovation — without changing your routine or your budget.

Mission

The Cellmetron Longevity Lab is our research division dedicated to transforming advanced longevity science into simple, enjoyable daily rituals. Our mission is clear: support cellular health in a way that fits naturally into real life — just like enjoying a cup of the best coffee or tea in the world, but even more affordable.

Research Includes:

  • Cellular Energy — NR pathways, NAD+ metabolism, mitochondrial resilience, ATP production
  • Antioxidant Defense — Resveratrol, Ergothioneine, oxidative stress biomarkers, sirtuin activation
  • Anti‑Senescence Pathways — Myricetin, cellular aging markers, mitochondrial protection
  • Metabolic Balance — Taurine, HRV, hydration, recovery, metabolic stability
  • Cognitive & Functional Longevity — Ergothioneine transporter pathways (OCTN1), neuro‑protection, cognitive clarity
  • Cardiovascular Support — Omega‑3 pathways, membrane integrity, inflammatory balance
  • Inflammation Modulation — Curcumin, NF‑κB pathways, cellular protection
  • Delivery System Innovation — cube compression, stability engineering, dissolution curves, flavor architecture, ritual design
  • Clinical Studies — observational studies, biomarker feasibility research, controlled trials, long‑term health outcomes
